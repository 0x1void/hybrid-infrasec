How I worked

  • Everything is real: the company is fictitious, the infrastructure is not. I built, operated and secured it myself.
  • What I claim, I prove. A fix counts only when the test that revealed the defect is replayed, from a third machine.
  • Logs are read in the raw, from the command line, not only in a dashboard.
  • A snapshot before every change, no secret in clear, rootless containers, least privilege.
